Crew Bios

Wiseman — Naval aviator and astronaut who logged 167 days in space on Expedition 41. Oldest human to travel beyond low Earth orbit.

Glover — Naval aviator who spent 168 days on the ISS as a Flight Engineer. First person of color and first active-duty US military member to live on the station long-term.

Koch — Holds the record for longest single spaceflight by a woman (328 days). Electrical engineer and climate researcher with 5 spacewalks.

Hansen — Canadian pilot and capcom for the ISS. Artemis II is his first spaceflight — first Canadian to travel beyond low Earth orbit.
